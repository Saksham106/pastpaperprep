# Biology 0610: extension material secondaries (metadata-only, finalized rows)

- Defect: `generate-igcse-biology-0610-extension-candidate.mjs` hard-coded `secondaryTopics: []` and `secondarySubtopics: []` for every 2019, 2020 and February/March 2026 row, although the pinned source assembly (`extension-2019-2020-2026/assembly/release/assembly.json`, SHA-256 `92132de0…`) records reviewed `classification.final.material_secondaries` for 200 of its 1,472 rows (338 secondary labels after de-duplication). The 2021–2025 rows (official-rebuild path) already carry their reviewed secondaries, so a topic filter matched a 2021–2025 question through a secondary but never a 2019/2020 one.
- Mapping: the same as the official-rebuild path. Taxonomy v2 lookup by era and normalized subtopic id (2026_2028 through the 2023_2025 registry, which has the same 61 addresses); `secondaryTopics` = parent topic titles, `secondaryTopicIds` = subtopic ids, `secondarySubtopics` = subtopic titles, and the subtopic titles appended to `subtopics`/`detailedSubtopics` after the primary entry. The same subtopic on several printed parts collapses to one entry (10 rows); a secondary equal to the primary is excluded (none occur). As on 2021–2025 rows, a secondary may share the primary's parent topic.
- Scope: only those five fields of exactly the 200 rows listed in `0610-extension-material-secondaries.json` (frozen cohort, re-derivable from the source with `--check`). Primary labels, marks, text, provenance, publication/classification states, asset references and object keys are unchanged; all other 4,713 rows are byte-identical.
- Seals: the storage manifest (`4984902a…`) and verified readback receipt (`2def1166…`) are untouched, so nothing is re-uploaded. The historical original candidate seal (`9e97cd0c…`) and the Pollution repair (#123) stay as recorded ancestry; `runtimeArtifact.secondaryTopicsRepair` records the baseline (runtime `f20c50d6…`, content `ea6aa083…`), the frozen cohort and the method, and `finalizedContentSha256` is resealed (`b72e0246…`). This follows the chained metadata-only precedent of 0620 and 0625.
- Visible effect: the projected student filters treat these rows like 2021–2025 rows. A secondary with a current 2026 heading adds that heading and topic; a 2019/2020 secondary heading with no current equivalent adds the "Earlier syllabus content" bucket (57 rows). Where a row's primary heading has no current equivalent, the projection shows its first current secondary topic as the displayed topic, as it already does for 2021–2025 rows (38 extension rows).
