# Tutor referrals — live pilot and payout runbook

**Technically live; not a legal-compliance sign-off.** Pietro's `/r/pietro` link is active in production. It gives the customer no discount. A signed HttpOnly first-party cookie retains the first valid click for 30 days on the same browser; after signup, verified attribution is stored against the account. Deleting cookies or changing devices before signup loses anonymous attribution. Only a first Stripe-collected paid subscription invoice can earn a commission: 30% of the amount actually collected excluding tax, after discounts. Renewals and out-of-band/zero-dollar invoices earn nothing.

## Monthly manual payout

After month-end, call `referral_monthly_payout_report('YYYY-MM-01')` with the service role. Its amounts are in minor currency units; currencies are separate. It holds a commission 30 days after recording before the first payout. Review underlying invoices, successful payments, refunds, disputes and recipient details in Stripe. Refunds or dispute changes after a prior payout appear as a negative carry-forward, not a second commission. A negative net is a debt to offset against a later payout, **not an instruction to send money**. An unresolved dispute holds the entire commission; a final **lost** dispute permanently forfeits it (`dispute_lost` is a zero-value final-status marker alongside the positive hold), while a **won** dispute adds a negative release. Never pay a held or lost commission.

Send and independently verify the external transfer manually. Then call `mark_referral_payout(month, partner_code, currency, expected_net_cents, transfer_reference)` with the exact reviewed nonnegative amount and a real unique transfer reference. It aborts if the report changed; exact repeats are idempotent. A zero-dollar fully adjusted row can be marked with a unique `no-transfer-...` reference after reconciliation. There is no automatic Stripe Connect payout. The service-role-only payout-batch table retains the transfer reference, month, and amount; reconcile it against the bank statement. If a late negative balance can never be offset, settle it manually before closing the pilot.

## Limitations

The current commercial implementation covers paid `subscription_create` invoices with a single successfully collected PaymentIntent. An invoice with multiple successful payments raises a retryable error and requires manual reconciliation rather than silently forfeiting a commission. If trials, setup fees, mixed credit/card invoices, or non-subscription products are introduced, add explicit eligibility tests and revisit the first-cash-payment rule before offering them through referrals. The 30-day window applies through payment. First-touch is only reliable on the same browser before account binding. Existing users cannot become new referred customers.
